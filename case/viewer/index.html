assembly
  1. uno onto its four standoffs, m3 self tapping
  2. half breadboard into the pocket, wire leds and resistors there
  3. leds pressed into the lid, arcade buttons from the top with their nuts
  4. lcd onto the four standoffs under the window, m2.5
  5. rc522 clipped into its frame, header toward the front slot, buzzer into its ring
  6. lid on, six m3 countersunk screws, rubber feet underneath
