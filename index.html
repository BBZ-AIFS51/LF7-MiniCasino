assembly
  1. uno onto its four standoffs, m3 self tapping
  2. half breadboard into the pocket, resistors and the contrast pot go there
  3. leds pressed into the lid, arcade buttons from the top with their nuts
  4. lcd onto the four standoffs under the window, m2.5
  5. rc522 clipped into its frame, header through the front slot, buzzer into its ring
  6. wire it like the model, then lid on with six m3 countersunk screws
